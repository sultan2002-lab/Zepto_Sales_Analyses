# Zepto Sales Analysis – MIS Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project is an Excel-based MIS Dashboard developed using a simulated retail sales dataset. The goal is to analyse sales performance, discounts, product costs, and profitability, and present meaningful business insights through an interactive-style dashboard.

## Objectives
- Analyse overall sales and business performance.
- Calculate Gross Sales, Net Sales, COGS, and Gross Profit.
- Compare sales performance across different cities and product categories.
- Analyse customer payment preferences.
- Present key business metrics through a dashboard.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel 2007
- Excel Formulas and Functions
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- KPI Cards
- Data Analysis and Reporting

## Excel Skills & Functions Used
- **Basic Functions:** SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNTA
- **Conditional Functions:** SUMIF, SUMIFS, COUNTIF, COUNTIFS, AVERAGEIF, AVERAGEIFS
- **Logical Functions:** IF, AND, OR, Nested IF
- **Lookup Functions:** VLOOKUP, HLOOKUP, INDEX-MATCH, IFERROR

## Key Project Features
- Data cleaning and organisation.
- Product information management using a Product Master sheet.
- Automated sales, discount, cost, and profit calculations.
- City-wise sales analysis.
- Category-wise sales analysis.
- Payment mode-wise sales analysis.
- Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts for business reporting.
- KPI-based dashboard for monitoring business performance.

## Project Structure
- **Cleaning_Data:** Cleaned sales transactions and calculated fields.
- **Product_Master:** Product information and illustrative unit costs.
- **Sales_Analysis:** Business calculations and summary metrics.
- **Pivot_Analysis:** City-wise, category-wise, and payment mode-wise reports.
- **Dashboard:** Visual presentation of KPIs and sales performance.

## Business Insights
The dashboard helps analyse:
- Which cities generate higher sales.
- Which product categories contribute more to revenue.
- How sales are distributed across payment methods.
- Overall sales and gross profitability.

## Important Note
This is an independent learning and portfolio project based on a simulated sales dataset. The purchase costs used for profitability calculations are illustrative assumptions for practice purposes. The project is not based on actual internal Zepto business data and is not affiliated with Zepto.
